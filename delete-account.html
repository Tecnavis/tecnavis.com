Legal / Account deletion

Delete your account

Effective 7 November 2026Last updated 7 November 2026Applies to tecnavis.com · our apps

You can delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time, from inside the App or by sending us a request.

On this page

  1. Delete from inside the App
  2. Request deletion without the App
  3. What is deleted and what is kept

01Delete from inside the App

The quickest way. In any Tecnavis App:

  1. Open the App and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile (or Settings) and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with the OTP or password we ask for. Your account is closed immediately.

02Request deletion without the App

If you have uninstalled the App or cannot sign in, send us a request. We verify it with an OTP or a reply from your registered email or phone, then delete your account within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Send request by email Send on WhatsApp

Opens your email app or WhatsApp with the request filled in. You can also write to info@tecnavis.com or message +91 62824 14031 directly.

03What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
ProfileName, phone, email, photo and password are deleted
Location historyDeleted
UploadsPhotos, documents and listings you posted are deleted
Device dataNotification tokens and device identifiers are deleted
Invoices and paymentsKept for up to 8 years where tax and accounting law requires, then deleted
Disputes or fraud casesKept only until the matter is closed
BackupsOverwritten within 90 days

Deletion is permanent. Order history, saved addresses and any wallet or subscription balance cannot be recovered. Cancel active subscriptions in Google Play or the App Store first, because deleting your account does not stop store billing.

To delete only some data and keep your account, say so in your request. See our Privacy Policy for more.
