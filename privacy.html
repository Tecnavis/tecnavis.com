01Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Tecnavis Web Solutions Pvt Ltd ("Tecnavis", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use:

  • our websites, including tecnavis.com and tecnavis.in;
  • mobile apps that we publish on Google Play and the Apple App Store under the developer name Tecnavis Web Solutions Pvt Ltd, and their related web dashboards ("Apps");
  • our own software products, such as Tecnavis Platform.

For personal data processed through these services, Tecnavis is the data controller (under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the "data fiduciary").

Apps we build for clients. If an app is published under a client's own developer account, that client is responsible for your data and its own privacy policy applies. Tecnavis then acts only as a service provider processing data on the client's instructions.

03How we use your information

  • To provide the service: create and manage your account, process orders and bookings, connect you with other users where the App is a marketplace, and send transactional messages such as OTPs, confirmations and status updates.
  • To keep it secure: verify identity, prevent fraud and abuse, and investigate misuse.
  • To support you: answer questions and resolve problems.
  • To improve it: fix crashes, measure performance and plan features, using aggregated or de-identified data where possible.
  • To communicate: send service notices. We send promotional messages only where you have agreed, and you can opt out at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations: tax, accounting and lawful requests from authorities.

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests (security and service improvement), your consent (such as location or marketing), and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

04Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed to run the service:

  • Other users of the same App, when sharing is part of the service, for example a customer's name, phone and pickup point shown to the assigned driver.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: cloud hosting, Google Firebase (authentication, push notifications, crash reporting), Google Maps Platform, SMS/OTP and email providers, and payment gateways such as Razorpay or Stripe.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect the rights, property or safety of users or the public.
  • A buyer or successor, if Tecnavis is involved in a merger or acquisition. Your data would stay protected by this policy.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies, for example Google and Razorpay.

06How long we keep it

We keep personal data while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except:

  • invoices and transaction records we must keep for tax and accounting law (up to 8 years under Indian law);
  • data needed to resolve a dispute, prevent fraud or enforce our terms, kept only until that matter is closed;
  • backups, which are overwritten on a rolling cycle of up to 90 days.

07Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your account and personal data (see Delete account);
  • withdraw consent, such as for location or marketing;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and receive your data in a portable format (EU/UK);
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (India).

Email info@tecnavis.com from the address or phone number linked to your account. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to your local data protection authority, for example the Data Protection Board of India, an EU supervisory authority or the UK ICO.

08Security

We protect data with HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access limited to staff who need it, and regular updates to our servers and dependencies. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

09International transfers

Tecnavis is based in India and serves clients in the GCC, Europe and elsewhere. Your data may be stored or processed in India or in the countries where our hosting and service providers operate. Where EU or UK data leaves those regions, we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10Children

Our services are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Where an App is designed for students, such as an educational App, users under 18 must use it with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian, and we do not use children's data for tracking or targeted advertising. If you believe a child has given us data without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the App or by email before they take effect.
